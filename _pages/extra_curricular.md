Apart from academics, I am passionate about music and painting. I did my elementary training of Hindustani Classical Music and Rabindra-sangeet, from a very young age, and continued until my high school days. I also got several opportunities to perform live on stage, in front of local audiences, however not all of them could get video recorded. Below are a few links to my performances during my undergraduate studies at Jadavpur University!
